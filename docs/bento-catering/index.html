Questions about bento and meal boxes

Can each box be labelled?

Yes. Tell us in the planner notes how you'd like boxes labelled, for example by dish or by vegetarian, and we'll label them for you.

Can I order different boxes in one order?

Yes. Choose any mix of dishes and options, such as chicken rice boxes, lamb wraps and falafel boxes.

Do you provide invoices for companies?

Yes. Mention that you need an invoice or a purchase order in the planner notes and we'll include the details in your quote.

How early should I order meal boxes?

For office orders we suggest at least 2 to 3 days ahead, and earlier for large numbers. Send your date through the planner and we'll confirm.
