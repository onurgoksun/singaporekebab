Our story

Singapore Kebab was started by Turks living in Singapore. We missed the kebab we grew up with: meat stacked on a vertical spit, carved thin while it is still crisp at the edges, and wrapped in fresh bread with a cool garlic yogurt sauce.

Singapore already loves kebab, so we asked a simple question. Why not bring the real thing to the moments people here celebrate together, from a Friday team lunch to a product launch or a wedding?

That is what we do. We cater Turkish kebab across Singapore, with live kebab carving at your venue or hot food delivered on time, ready to serve.

What we care about

100% halal

All our food is halal and prepared in our MUIS halal-certified kitchen in Little India. Every guest at your table can enjoy the same meal.

Real Turkish kebab

Chicken and lamb kebab cooked on a vertical spit and carved to order, the way it is done in Turkey. Not generic shawarma.

Cooked fresh on the day

Your order is prepared on the day of your event and delivered hot, or carved live at your venue.

Clear prices

Our menu prices are on the website. The planner shows an estimate as you build your menu, so there are no surprises.

Something for every guest

Falafel, hummus, salads and vegetarian pide sit next to the kebab, so mixed groups are covered.

What we cater

Live kebab carving for parties, launches and weddings, where our chef carves to order in front of your guests. Office catering with buffet trays or individual meal boxes for team lunches and dinners. Wedding catering with a full Turkish table. You can also order from our menu for smaller groups.
