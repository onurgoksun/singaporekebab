Questions about wedding catering

Can we mix a live station with a buffet?

Yes. Many couples pair a live kebab station with a buffet of meze, pilav and desserts. Tell us what you have in mind in the planner.

Can you cater for guests with dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us about vegetarian guests, allergies and any other requirements in the planner, and we'll confirm what we can provide in your quote.

How early should we book for a wedding?

As early as possible, especially for weekend dates. Send your date through the planner and we'll confirm availability.
