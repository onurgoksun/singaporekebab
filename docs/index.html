Two ways to cater

Choose live carving for the show, or drop-off when you just need everyone fed well.

Live carving at your event

Our chef brings the döner spit and carves to order in front of your guests. Wraps and plates are made fresh, one after another.

  • Chef and carving station set up at your venue
  • Wraps and rice plates made to order
  • Sauces, salads and sides on the counter

Best for company events, parties, weddings and open houses.

Drop-off catering

We prepare everything in our kitchen and deliver it hot, at the time you set. Laid out buffet-style or packed in individual boxes.

  • Delivered at your chosen time
  • Buffet trays or individual meal boxes
  • Plates, cutlery and napkins included on request

Best for office lunches, team dinners, meetings and training days.

Made for groups

Döner is easy to serve, easy to eat standing up, and works for most diets with chicken, beef and vegetarian options.
