Catering questions, answered

How do I get a quote?

Use the menu planner. It takes about a minute: you tell us your guest count, date, service style, dishes and area, and your request opens in WhatsApp ready to send. We reply with a menu and price.

What is live kebab carving?

Our kebab master sets up a vertical spit at your venue and carves to order. Guests get fresh wraps or plates with salads and sauces. We check space, power and timing with you before the day.

Is there a minimum number of guests?

We cater small team lunches as well as large events. Tell us your number in the planner and we'll suggest the best format for it.

Is your food halal?

Yes. All our food is halal. Everything is prepared in our MUIS halal-certified kitchen in Little India, so you can serve it to all your guests with confidence. We also have vegetarian dishes such as falafel, hummus and veggie moussaka.

Do you deliver across Singapore?

Tell us your area or postal code in the planner. We'll confirm delivery and any delivery fee in your quote.

Can my company pay by invoice?

Yes. Let us know in the planner notes if you need an invoice or a purchase order, and we'll include the details in your quote.

Can I change the menu?

Yes. Every menu is built for your event, so you can swap dishes, adjust portions or add desserts and drinks before you confirm.
