Questions about christmas catering

When should I book Christmas catering?

As early as possible. December weekends and the days before Christmas are the busiest dates of the year.

Can you do live kebab carving at an office party?

Yes. We check space, power, ventilation and building rules with you before the day.

Do you cater Christmas parties at home?

Yes. We deliver hot buffets to homes across Singapore, ready to serve.

Do you provide invoices for company parties?

Yes. Mention that you need an invoice or a purchase order in the planner notes.
