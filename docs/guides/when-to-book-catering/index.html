When should you book catering in Singapore?

How far ahead to book office lunches, live carving and wedding catering in Singapore, which seasons get busy, and a simple planning timeline.

By Singapore Kebab. Updated 25 October 2026

The short answer: the bigger and more special the event, the earlier you should book. Here's a simple guide for planning catering in Singapore.

How far ahead to book

Type of eventBook at leastIdeally
Office lunch or meeting2 to 3 days ahead1 week ahead
Company party or launch1 to 2 weeks ahead3 to 4 weeks ahead
Live kebab carving1 to 2 weeks ahead3 to 4 weeks ahead
Wedding or large celebration1 to 2 months ahead3 months or more

Singapore caterers often ask for at least two days' notice for buffets, and live carving needs extra time to plan staff, equipment and the venue. These are general guides: send your date through the planner and we'll confirm what's possible.

Busy seasons in Singapore

Book earlier for events around these periods, when caterers fill up fast:

  • November to December: year-end parties, Christmas and team celebrations
  • Chinese New Year: lo hei lunches and open houses in January or February
  • Hari Raya and Deepavali: open houses and office celebrations
  • Weekends and public holidays: especially for weddings and live carving

A simple planning timeline

  1. 4 to 6 weeks before: set your date, rough headcount and budget per guest. Book your caterer for big events.
  2. 2 to 3 weeks before: choose your menu and service style: live carving, buffet or meal boxes. Check venue access, space and power.
  3. 1 week before: collect dietary needs and confirm the serving time and delivery address.
  4. 2 to 3 days before: confirm your final headcount and any changes.
  5. On the day: keep someone reachable for delivery, check the consume-by labels and serve on time.

What to have ready when you ask for a quote

  • Date and serving time
  • Number of guests and any dietary needs
  • Live carving, buffet or meal boxes
  • Venue address, and notes on lifts, loading bays or building rules
  • Budget per guest and whether you need an invoice or PO

Quick answers

How many days in advance should I order catering in Singapore?

For office lunches, aim for at least 2 to 3 days. For live carving and parties, 1 to 2 weeks or more. For weddings, 1 to 3 months. Book earlier in busy seasons like December and Chinese New Year.

When should I confirm the final headcount?

Two to three days before the event is a good rule, so the caterer can prepare the right quantities.
