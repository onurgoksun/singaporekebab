Questions about party catering

Can you cater a party at home?

Yes. We deliver buffets to homes across Singapore. For live kebab carving we check space, power and ventilation with you before the day.

Do you have food for kids?

Yes. Chicken nuggets with fries and simple kebab wraps are popular with kids.

How far ahead should I book party catering?

As early as you can, especially for weekends. For live carving, 1 to 2 weeks ahead is a good guide.

Can I see the prices first?

Yes. Every dish has its price on our menu page, and the planner shows a running estimate as you build your menu.
