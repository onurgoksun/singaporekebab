What goes into a kebab mini buffet

A mini buffet gives a small group the feel of a full spread without ordering for a hundred people. A typical Turkish kebab buffet from us includes:

  • Chicken or lamb kebab, or a mix of both
  • Buttery rice pilaf or warm pita bread
  • Shepherd's salad and hummus
  • Garlic yoghurt and chilli sauces
  • Falafel for vegetarian guests
  • Baklava or kunafa to finish

You choose the dishes and the quantities, so the buffet fits your group, not a fixed package.

How much should you order?

Plan one main portion per guest, then add sides and a little extra if your group has big eaters. Our guide on how much food to order per person walks you through it, and the menu planner counts mains against your guest number as you build the menu.

Every dish has its price on our menu page, and the planner shows a running estimate as you add dishes. We confirm delivery and the final total with you on WhatsApp.

Questions about mini buffet catering

What size of group is a mini buffet for?

Mini buffets work well for small groups such as team lunches, birthdays and house gatherings. Tell us your number in the planner and we'll suggest the right quantities.

Is the food delivered hot?

Yes. Your buffet is cooked on the day and delivered hot at the time you set, ready to serve.

Can I mix chicken and lamb?

Yes. You choose how many portions of each dish and each option, so some guests can have chicken and others lamb.

Do you have vegetarian dishes for a buffet?

Yes. Falafel, hummus, salads, veggie moussaka and cheese or spinach pide all work well on a buffet.
