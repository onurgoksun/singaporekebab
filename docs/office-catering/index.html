Questions about office catering

Is your food halal?

Yes. All our food is halal. Everything is prepared in our MUIS halal-certified kitchen in Little India, so you can serve it to all your guests with confidence.

Is there a minimum order for office catering?

We cater small team lunches as well as large company events. Tell us your number in the planner and we'll suggest the best format.

Can you deliver to my office?

Tell us your building or postal code in the planner. We'll confirm delivery time and any delivery fee in your quote.

Do you provide invoices for companies?

Yes. Mention that you need an invoice or a purchase order in the planner notes and we'll include the details in your quote.

Can you cater for vegetarian colleagues?

Yes. We have falafel, hummus, salads and veggie moussaka, and every order comes with clear dietary labels.
